# HV Trio box on market positions 4-10

## Question

Skip the three favourites: box market positions 4-10 for a Trio in HV races with 12+ runners.
Bet only races with **avgDiff <= 10**. Does fading the top of the market pay, and which avgDiff
cut-off is best?

## Method

- Universe: `data/racecards/racecard_*_HV_R*.json` with a finish order in `data/historical/`.
- Funnel: 338 races with results → 283 with ≥12 non-scratched runners → **118** with avgDiff <= 10.
  All 283 are 12-runner races; HV fields do not exceed 12 in this data.
- avgDiff: `round(mean(|topRating − rating|))` from `FormAnalyzer.analyzeRace`. avgDiff is an integer,
  so `<= 10` selects the same races as `< 11`.
- Market position: rank by win odds ascending, 1 = favourite. Odds are the starting price from the
  results file, falling back to the racecard snapshot; ties broken by saddle-cloth number.
- Bet: box market positions **4-10** (7 horses) = C(7,3) = 35 combos × $10 = **$350 per race**.
- Hit: all three placed finishers inside those 7 (`winningCombos(order, 3)`, dead-heat aware).
- Payout: actual `trioDividend` (per $10). No Monte Carlo — selection is pure market rank, so the
  result is deterministic and seed-free.

## Result

The chosen rule, avgDiff <= 10:

| Group | Races | Hits | Hit rate | Stake | Payout | P&L | ROI |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **All** | 118 | 14 | 11.9% | $41,300 | $50,437 | **+$9,137** | **+22.1%** |
| Without the single best race | 117 | 13 | 11.1% | $40,950 | $39,658 | −$1,292 | −4.0% |

Dividend economics: median winning dividend **$3,629**, mean **$3,603**, max **$10,779**;
break-even at an 11.9% hit rate needs a mean of **$2,950**.

Winning dividends: $693, $1,796, $1,953, $2,138, $2,196, $2,683, $2,774, $3,629, $4,010, $4,092,
$4,108, $4,149, $5,437, $10,779.

## Interpretation

- **The economics invert the MC box.** Fading the favourites cuts the hit rate from 34.7% to 11.9%,
  but lifts the average winning dividend from $807 to $3,603 against the $2,950 needed. Rare hits,
  fat payouts.
- **One race supplies the profit.** 2026-02-25 R4 paid $10,779 (+$10,429). Remove it and the rule is
  −4.0%. With 14 hits in 118 races a 17-race losing run (−$7,707 peak-to-trough) is normal.
- **Tightening to <= 9 does not help — it removes the good bucket.** The avgDiff-10 bucket holds the
  $10,779 payout; cutting it leaves a rule that lands exactly on cost (mean dividend $2,878 vs $2,870
  needed) and would have sat 20 races deep in a losing run.
- **<= 8 is the most robust cell tested.** It is the only cut-off still positive after removing its
  best race (+21.6%), and it has the highest hit rate (16.3%) on the fewest races (49, 8 hits).
- **Loosening past 10 kills it**: <= 12 is −6.1% and no filter is −32.4%. The filter's job is to avoid
  races where favourites are solid.
- What the rule really bets on is all three favourites missing the frame — 14/118 = 11.9% here.

## Caveats

- 14 hits. Nothing is statistically settled; ROI swings from +22.1% to −4.0% on one race.
- Fat-tailed payouts: the mean dividend is dominated by the maximum.
- Bankroll reality: expect runs of 15-20 losers and a $7,000-$12,000 drawdown at $350 a race.
- Starting prices come from the results file, else snapshot odds. No race in this set lacked prices.
- Scratchings as recorded on the racecard; positions computed after removing scratched runners.
- Class and month splits rest on 0-4 hits each and should not drive decisions yet.

## Action taken

None. Recorded for comparison against the MC-based Trio work.

## Recommendation (open)

- Run the race-by-race for **avgDiff <= 8** — the only cut-off that survives losing its best race.
- Test the same rule on ST (fields up to 14); HV caps at 12, so the "12+" condition never binds here.
- Vary the window: 4-9, 5-10, 4-11, and a 6-horse version.
- Test the complement (positions 1-3 plus a longshot) to confirm the payoff comes from chalk failing.
- Re-run once another 50-100 HV races exist; 14 hits cannot separate edge from luck.
