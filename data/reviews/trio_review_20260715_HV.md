## Section 8: Learnings

**What Worked**
- **Mode C wide pool on a flat race (R3) caught a $1,942 frame** — the lean-PASS race delivered the whole profit. Evidence that wide nets on genuinely open HV races can pay when a coverable anchor (market fav #10) holds.
- **R6 thin-banker warning was correct** — #7 DOUBLE ALPHA (2 form entries, course debut) flagged as weak banker, finished 10th. Honouring the flag (PASS) saved nothing extra since we noted lean-PASS, but validates the rule.
- MC/market divergence R3 (#5 SERANGOON, MC 44% vs market 20) resolved MC's way — the model's all-form Place% found a live 33-1 placer.

**What Didn't Work**
- Banker 4/9 again — two straight cold meetings.
- The three "core" recommendations (R5, R7, R8) all missed; the flagged lean-PASS R3 hit. Core-selection heuristic (dominant + market-aligned) did NOT correlate with hits this meeting.
- R1 near-Pattern-A: #3+#11 in legs, banker #9 missed — banker structure cost a hit that a full pool would have caught.

**Strategy Adjustments**
- [ ] **Reconsider banker structure on wide/flat HV races** — R1 would have hit with a full pool. When no horse has Adj Win% ≥ 25%, evaluate a small full-pool (no banker) or 雙膽 alternative rather than anchoring one 44%-place horse.
- [ ] **Do not down-weight Mode C lean-PASS races to zero** — R3 shows the wide net can return the meeting. If playing, the Mode C 7-pool is the correct structure.
- [ ] Continue folding the odds<10 swap (carry from 12/07) — neutral this meeting.
- [ ] Two cold-banker meetings (45%, 44%) — monitor; still within variance vs ~56% season norm, do not overfit.
