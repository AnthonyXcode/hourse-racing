## Section 8: Learnings

**What Worked**
- The R7 divergence PASS-warning was vindicated (market beat MC 40%). Honouring it would have saved $60 and avoided a false-confidence banker.
- R3 B-variant swap (#9 in for #8 on odds<10) was the *only* winning structure — evidence the MC-only odds swap adds value on short-priced live horses.

**What Didn't Work**
- Banker 5/11 wipeout — no meeting survives 6 banker failures.
- Strategy A's tighter Mode-A pools (R1, R6, R7, R9, R11) excluded the exact gap horses; even Mode-B 6-pools (R2,R5) missed at short prices.
- Deep longshots detonated: #10@97 (R6), #6@93 (R7), #12@68 (R4) — outside any rational pool. Nothing to fix there.

**Strategy Adjustments**
- [ ] **Enforce the divergence PASS rule**: when MC #1 Win% ≥2× the market-implied prob, do NOT bank it — PASS or bank the market fav instead. R7 would have been skipped.
- [ ] **Adopt the MC-only odds swap into Strategy A** (add any Place%≤20 but odds<10 horse): R3 proves it. Keep #9-type live shorties in the A pool.
- [ ] **Widen Mode-A pools to 6 when the 5th/6th MC horse is <10 odds** — R1 (#11@4.5) and R9 (#5@12) gaps were exactly these.
- [ ] Do not chase: a 0/11 meeting is variance, not a structural break — banker top-3 45% is a cold sample, season norm is higher.
