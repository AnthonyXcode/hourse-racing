## Rules

**Strategy B (review-spec, used for the headline numbers):**
- **Banker** = MC #1 by raw MC Win% (no debutant rule, no SCMP, no jockey boost)
- **Legs** = MC #2–#6 by raw MC Win%
- **Unit** $10, structure 膽拖 1膽 + 5腳, C(5,2) = **10 combos = $100**, all races played
- **Hit** = banker top 3 AND all 3 placers in the 6-horse pool

**B-trio (reports' own MC-only ticket, for continuity):**
- **Banker** = MC #1; **primary legs** = every other horse with MC Place% > 20%
- **Step B** = any horse with MC Place% ≤ 20% AND Win odds < 10 replaces the lowest-Place% primary leg that is 20–30% with odds > 10; otherwise it is **added**
- **Unit** $10, 膽拖, combos = C(N,2)

1. **B lost $290 less than A only because it staked less.** Both went 0/9 with the same bankers. In R5 the late scratchings cut B's stake to $30.

The card argues for testing a **swap-only** market rule inside B (replace L5 with the shortest-priced horse below MC #6 when it is under 10) rather than adopting B-trio wholesale.
4. **A 7th leg (B7) would not have helped.** It would have added R3 #1, R5 #5 and R9 #6, but each of those races also had a banker failure or a second gap.
5. **The banker is still the bottleneck: 3/9, and 0/4 below 30% MC Win%.** Two-card record below 30% is 0/8. A Competitive-race PASS for MC #1 < 30% would have saved $330 on this card ($100 R1, $30 R5, $100 R8, $100 R9) for no lost hits.
