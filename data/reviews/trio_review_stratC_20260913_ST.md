**Pattern C dominated at 6/9**, but the single **Pattern A** race is the most instructive on the card. In **R9 all three placers — #5 LUCY IN THE SKY (1st), #1 CHILL BUDDY (2nd), #2 AURORA PATCH (3rd) — were sitting in B-trio's leg pool.** The ticket lost only because banker #8 THUNDER KIT, locked into all 10 combos, ran 8th. The Step B Win-odds rule had already done its job: it **added #1 CHILL BUDDY** (MC Place% 18.8%, below the 20% leg bar, but Win odds < 10), and #1 ran 2nd. A full C(6,3)=20 box of the same six horses would have cost $200 and returned $159 — still a loss, so even the box does not rescue it; the banker concept itself is what failed.

Strategy A's pattern split differs (B: R5, R10 / C: 7 races) because A's debutant rule gave it a **better** banker in R5 (#10, 3rd) than B-trio's MC #1 (#9, 8th), and a **worse** one in R8 (#4 at 19, ran 7th, vs #1 at 1.5, ran 2nd). A never reached Pattern A because its R9 pool excluded #1 CHILL BUDDY — the horse B-trio's Win-odds rule correctly added.

**Noteworthy misses:**

- **R8** — B-trio's banker #1 SOLID STATE was the 1.5 favourite and ran 2nd exactly as modelled (MC Place% 50.6%, market implied ~80%). The race was lost to **two** horses outside a 7-horse pool: winner #10 ALPHA STRIKE at 11 (market's **2nd** choice, MC Place% 19.4% — just under the 20% leg threshold) and #11 CONGHUA GALAXY at 29. The 20% cut-off excluded the market's second favourite by 0.6pp.
- **R10** — banker #12 NYX GLUCK won and #2 AEROVOLANIC (leg) ran 2nd. B-trio's 8-horse pool cost $210 and still missed #5 CHIU CHOW SPIRIT (20, market 8th, MC Place% 20.6%). Widening from 5 to 7 legs bought nothing; the miss was one specific horse the model rated 9th.
- **R5** — the Win-odds Step B rule **added** #11 BETTER AND BETTER (6.2) and #7 GHORGAN (10) as extra legs for $40. Neither placed (5th, 9th). The horse that did place at 36 — #12 NOBLE SUPERIOR — was invisible to both the MC (5.9% Place) and the market (12th).
- **R2** — banker #7 HERO MASTERMIND was MC #1 at 22.7% off **one career start** and went off at **83** in the market. He ran 7th. This is the clearest case on the card of the MC rating a horse it has no data for.

## Conclusions for Strategy B

1. **The banker rule is the binding constraint, not the leg rule.** B-trio's legs contained 2 of 3 placers in 9 of 10 races and all 3 in R9. The 膽 killed seven tickets.
2. **The Step B Win-odds rule earned its keep again.** It added #1 CHILL BUDDY in R9 (ran 2nd) and #5 LUCKY YEAR in R6 (ran 2nd) — both below the 20% Place bar, both under 10 in the market. Both were wasted by banker failures, exactly as on 09-Sep where three of four correct Win-odds calls were wasted the same way.
3. **The MC Place% > 20% leg threshold is mis-set.** R7's winner #12 ABSOLUTE AWAKENED was excluded at **19.7%** — 0.3pp under the bar — on a race that paid $18,953. R8's winner #10 ALPHA STRIKE was the market's 2nd choice at 6.5% Place.
4. **Wider is not better.** B-trio's two most expensive tickets (R3 $210, R10 $210) both missed, and R10 missed by a horse the 8-horse pool still did not reach. Stake went up 33% against Strategy A for identical returns.
5. **Model calibration on bankers is off by ~36pp** (66.2% mean predicted place vs 30.0% actual). Until that is re-fitted, every 膽拖 cost table is wrong.
