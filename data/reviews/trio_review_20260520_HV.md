## Section 8: Learnings

### What Worked

- **Tight Mode A pools on dominant races (R7–R8)** — R8 hit with only $60 staked; banker #3 at 45% MC Win delivered a top-3 finish.
- **Six-horse Mode B framing (R6)** — all three placers were MC-tier horses; banker leg structure worked when #3 placed.
- **Pool coverage on competitive 1000m/1200m races** — 8/9 races had ≥2 placers in pool; probability sorting was directionally sound.
- **R8 wide-trip excuse on #6 FLYING WROTE** — +2% adjustment; horse won at 5.3 SP.

### What Didn't Work

- **Banker selection on HV 1000m chaos (R1, R5)** — two Pattern A misses with **$998** left on table; market favourites (#7, #2) beat MC bankers.
- **Banker ≠ MC #1 on co-leader races (R3)** — #6 vs #10 split (0.5% gap) picked wrong banker; winner #2 was invisible to MC.
- **Favourite blindness (R4)** — #6 FORTUNE STAR (32.5% MC) 5th while 1.7 SP #8 DECISION LINK won; #10 NORTHERN BEAST 2nd outside pool.
- **Class 2 longshot (R9)** — #8 VICTOR THE WINNER (0.3% MC, 59 SP) won; no disciplined pool would include it.
- **R7 pool cap excluded #4 POWER OF VITAM** (27.9% Adj Place%, must-include threshold) — placer finished 2nd.

### Strategy Adjustments

- [ ] **Pattern A banker fallback:** when ≥3 legs have Adj Place% > 40% and banker is <30% Win, consider 雙膽拖 or promote MC #2 as co-banker on HV sprints.
- [ ] **HV 1000m market-fav check:** if a leg is market favourite (SP <4) but not banker, flag for banker review (R1 #7, R5 #2).
- [ ] **Must-include override for Mode A:** when excluded horse is ≥25% Adj Place% and finishes in top 3 historically at HV, widen to Mode B (R7 #4).
- [ ] **Cap tickets on MC Win% <5% fields:** R9-style 0.3% winners are unmodelable — consider PASS when top horse <25% Win and field entropy high (Class 2+).
- [ ] **Scrape R10+ before review:** R10 failed parse (going condition); fix scraper for full-card reviews.
