## 11 · Learnings

### What Worked

1. **Banker selection for G1 races was strong (2/3 won):** R5 KA YING RISING and R9 ROMANTIC WARRIOR both won convincingly, proving MC handles local G1 stars well.
2. **Strategy B's wider pool paid off in R11:** The inclusion of #5 WINNING OVATION (market favourite at 4.0x) was the difference between a $0 and $176 return.
3. **Pool construction correctly identified value legs in R2, R8, R9:** All three placers were inside the pool — the bet structure (double-banker) was the failure, not the horse selection.

### What Didn't Work

1. **Banker rate collapsed to 27.3% (campaign avg ~49%):** The MC model overrated several horses — R2 #4 AESTHETICISM (9th, 28x), R3 #3 HAYDAY (5th, 20x), R10 #9 THE GOLDEN KNIGHT (11th, 7.1x). These were not close calls — they ran badly.
2. **雙膽拖 went 0/5 — zero double-banker tickets would have hit:** Even with perfect legs, no 雙膽拖 race had both bankers in the top 3. The structure is too restrictive for volatile Champion's Day fields.
3. **International G1 fields broke the model:** R5's 2nd and 3rd were 90x and 345x overseas raiders. R7's top 3 included international horses MC couldn't calibrate. R9's 2nd/3rd were Japanese and French runners.
4. **Mode A (tight pool) continues to underperform:** 7/11 races used Mode A, producing 0 hits. The pool was simply too narrow for a 14-runner Champion's Day card.

### Strategy Adjustments

- [ ] **Consider skipping or reducing stakes for G1 international races:** 3 G1 races cost $190 (A) / $230 (B) for $0 return. MC cannot model overseas raiders. A "skip G1" rule saves ~$200/meeting on Champion's Day.
- [ ] **Review 雙膽拖 trigger criteria:** 0/5 hit rate today, campaign rate declining. Consider requiring both bankers to have >15% MC Place% AND co-banker odds < 5.0x before deploying 雙膽拖.
- [ ] **Investigate banker selection failures:** 3 primary bankers finished outside top 5 (R2 9th, R10 11th, R4 7th). These were not marginal misses — MC was fundamentally wrong. Review form weight decay and class-change adjustments.
- [ ] **Expand Mode A to Mode B universally:** The data now shows Mode A underperforms Mode B consistently. The extra $40-100 per race in stakes is offset by the improved hit rate from wider coverage.
